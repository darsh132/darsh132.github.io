FAQ

Before you purchase.

Is QuoteDesk self-hosted?

Yes. QuoteDesk is designed for organizations that want to deploy the application within their own infrastructure.

Can I customize quotation templates?

Template customization and branding capabilities depend on the edition and features included in the purchased license.

Can I add my company logo?

The product is designed around company branding and document personalization.

Can multiple employees use QuoteDesk?

Yes. Commercial editions are intended for internal business teams. Exact user limits will be defined by the selected license.

Does the license allow resale?

Standard commercial licensing does not grant redistribution, resale or white-label rights unless explicitly included in a separate agreement.

Can you build custom quotation functionality?

Yes. Custom development, integrations and organization-specific workflows can be discussed separately.
