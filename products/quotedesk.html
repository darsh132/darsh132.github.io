FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What can QuoteDesk be used for?

QuoteDesk is intended for businesses that need to create structured, professional quotations and proposals for customers.

Can I customize quotation templates?

QuoteDesk is designed around reusable quotation layouts and configurable business branding. Exact customization capabilities depend on the version and license purchased.

Can I add my company logo?

The product is designed to support business branding, including company identity and document branding.

Does QuoteDesk generate PDFs?

PDF document generation is one of the core capabilities planned for the commercial product.

Can I deploy QuoteDesk on my own server?

Yes. QuoteDesk is designed as a self-hosted application and can be deployed within an appropriate Windows/IIS environment.

Can I resell QuoteDesk?

A standard commercial license does not automatically grant resale or redistribution rights. Separate licensing would be required.
