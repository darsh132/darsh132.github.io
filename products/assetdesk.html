FAQ

Before you purchase.

Is AssetDesk self-hosted?

Yes. AssetDesk is designed for organizations that want to deploy the application within their own infrastructure.

What type of assets can I track?

AssetDesk can be used for IT equipment, office equipment, furniture and other physical business assets.

Can assets be assigned to employees?

Yes. Asset assignment is one of the core workflows of the application.

Can I track different office locations?

Location management can be used to organize assets across offices, departments or other business sites.

Can I customize AssetDesk?

Customization and additional organization-specific functionality can be discussed separately.

Can I resell the software?

Standard licensing does not include redistribution, resale or white-label rights unless specifically agreed under a separate license.
