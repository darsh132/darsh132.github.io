FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is AssetDesk?

AssetDesk is designed to help organizations maintain a centralized record of company equipment and track its assignment and status.

Who is AssetDesk for?

It is intended for businesses, offices and organizations that need a structured way to manage internal equipment and assets.

Can I host AssetDesk myself?

Yes. The product is designed around a self-hosted deployment model.

Can assets be assigned to employees?

Yes. Employee assignment is one of the core workflows of the application.

Can I customize AssetDesk?

Customization capabilities depend on the specific edition and license. Enterprise customization can be offered separately.

Can I resell AssetDesk?

Standard commercial licensing does not automatically include redistribution or resale rights. Separate licensing would be required.
