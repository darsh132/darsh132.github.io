FAQ

Questions before buying.

Is FollowUpDesk cloud based?

FollowUpDesk is designed primarily as a self-hosted application, allowing organizations to deploy it within their own infrastructure.

Can I deploy it on IIS?

Yes. The application is designed for ASP.NET Core deployment on Windows/IIS environments.

Can multiple employees use it?

The commercial edition is intended for internal business teams. The exact user and deployment limits depend on the selected license.

Do I receive the source code?

Source-code availability depends on the license edition purchased. The commercial license terms will specify the exact rights included.

Can you customize FollowUpDesk?

Yes. Custom development and business-specific modifications can be discussed separately.

Can I resell the software?

Standard commercial licensing does not automatically grant redistribution, white-label or resale rights. Those rights require a separate agreement.
