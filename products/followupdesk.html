Overview

A focused workflow for customer follow-ups.

FollowUpDesk is designed for teams that need visibility over customers, pending follow-ups and sales activity without adopting an unnecessarily complex CRM platform.

Maintain structured customer information and keep important business relationships in one place.

Follow-Up Scheduling
